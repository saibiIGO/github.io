📖 About Our School

Located in Innoshima, Onomichi City, Saibi-Juku is an all-age, self-paced Go school welcoming everyone from children to adults. We foster deep thinking skills through the beautiful game of Go, combining local classes with a vibrant online community.

🌐 Play & Learn Online

Online Go Arena "INGO"

A free online Go arena open to everyone! Play at your own pace, across time zones and language barriers. Perfect for players of all ages and skill levels.

Community Square "Discord"

Our online communication hub where players of all generations gather. Connect, chat, and review games with friends and teachers in real-time.
