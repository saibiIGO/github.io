🎁 Recommended Go AI & Useful Tools (English Version)

① Go AI & Game Analysis: "AI Sensei"

➔ https://ai-sensei.com

[Mobile Friendly] Click the blue link above to open the website. This platform allows you to upload your own game records (SGF) to let KataGo analyze your mistakes and show winning percentages. It natively supports English navigation as well.

② "Online KataGo" (Web Version)

➔ https://onlinekatago.net

[No Registration Required / PC Recommended] An online Go board running KataGo in your browser. Please note that the board layout may get cut off on mobile devices, so opening it on a desktop PC or a large monitor is highly recommended.

③ Standalone PC Software: "Sabaki" / "KaTrain"

➔ Download Sabaki (An elegant, highly customizable Go board interface)
➔ Download KaTrain (A popular, easy-to-use application with KataGo integrated)

[PC Only] These links take you to the download pages for installing software locally on your computer (Windows, Mac, or Linux). Ideal for players who want to set up a robust local AI review environment.

④ Game Record Editor: "Kiin Editor"

➔ Official Download Page (The Nihon Ki-in Website)

[Windows PC Only] Free and safe software officially provided by the Japan Go Association (Nihon Ki-in). It allows users to record, save, and manage game files, study professional match records, and print out clean, formatted diagrams.
